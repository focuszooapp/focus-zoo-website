Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Overview

Focus Zoo is a focus timer app developed by the Focus Zoo Team. This privacy policy explains how Focus Zoo handles your data.

Data Collection

Focus Zoo does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app does not require an internet connection, account registration, or any personal information to function.

Local Storage

Focus Zoo stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Paw balance
  • Unlocked animals
  • Selected active animal
  • Focus session history (date, length and which animal you focused with), used for hats, crowns and the Stats screen
  • Earned and equipped hats
  • App settings

Deleting the app removes all of this data.

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by the developer or any third party.

Third Party Services

Focus Zoo does not use any third party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Focus Zoo does not collect any data from users of any age.

Changes to This Policy

Any future changes to this privacy policy will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at focuszoodev@gmail.com.
